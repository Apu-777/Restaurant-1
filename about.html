How it began

One hearth, one table, twelve seats.

Ember & Salt opened in 2016 as a twelve-seat room built around a single oak-fired hearth. We had no grand concept. We wanted to cook food that tasted of the ingredient, and to serve it warmly.

Today the room is bigger, but the idea hasn’t moved. Short menu. Local farms. Fire doing most of the work.

What we believe

Four small rules.

  • 01

    Fire first

    Everything touches flame, even desserts. It’s our signature and our discipline.

  • 02

    Know the farmer

    If we can’t name who grew it, it doesn’t go on the menu.

  • 03

    Edit, then edit again

    A short menu means every plate has to earn its place.

  • 04

    Hospitality is the dish

    Good food forgotten by bad service is still a bad night. We train for both.
