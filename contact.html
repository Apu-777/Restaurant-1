Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, the bar and six tables are kept for walk-ins every evening. Weekend waits can run long, so booking is wise.

Can you cater to dietary needs?

Almost always. Tell us when you book and the kitchen will plan around you. Vegetarian and gluten-free dishes are marked on the menu.

Is there a dress code?

None. Come as you are, though the room can get warm near the hearth.

How large a group can you seat?

Up to twelve at our long table. For larger parties, write to our events team and we’ll arrange a set menu.

Do you host private events?

Yes, from intimate dinners to weddings. See our portfolio for past events.
