Ember & Salt

The chef

Arman Rahim, keeper of the fire.

Head chef & founder

Cooking is mostly listening.

Arman trained in Dhaka, Lyon and Copenhagen before coming home to build a kitchen around a single wood fire. He still lights it himself every afternoon.

His approach is simple: choose exceptional produce, apply the right amount of heat, then step back. The skill is knowing when to stop.
